# 🏥 Healthcare & MedTech QA Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

> [!NOTE]
> Verify against current company QMS procedures and applicable regulations.

## 📋 Essential Regulatory Standards
* **FDA 21 CFR Part 820**: Medical Device Quality System Regulation (Design Controls).
* **FDA 21 CFR Part 11**: Electronic Records & Signatures (Immutable audit trails).
* **HIPAA**: Protection of Protected Health Information (PHI masking & encryption).

## 🔒 Verification vs Validation Rule
* **Verification**: *"Did we build the software output to match SRS specifications?"* (Static reviews, unit/integration testing).
* **Validation**: *"Does the software meet intended user needs safely in actual clinical operation?"* (UAT, clinical workflow testing).
